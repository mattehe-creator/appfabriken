# Privacy policy — Warranty Vault

Last updated: 2026-10-13

Warranty Vault is developed by TM Connect. Contact: [contact address added at publication].

## In short

Everything you save in Warranty Vault stays on your phone. We have no server, no account, no ads and no analytics. The app does not request the internet permission and therefore cannot send anything by itself. We do not receive, store or share any personal data.

## What is stored, and where

The following is stored only locally on your phone, in the app's own storage:

- Your purchases: item, store, date, price, warranty period, notes, return window and item type.
- Receipts and photos of faults that you take or choose.
- The store's contact details, if you or the receipt provide them (email, phone, organisation number). Personal ID numbers are never stored.
- Fault descriptions and the date you made a complaint.
- Settings, such as reminders and the backup interval.

The receipt is read on the phone (on-device text recognition). The image and the text never leave the phone in that process.

## When data leaves the phone

Only when you choose it yourself:

- **Export and backup.** You save a file through the system file picker, to a place you decide, for example in your own cloud service. Automatic backup is off by default and only writes to the folder you choose.
- **Complaint email.** The app prepares an email that opens in your mail app. You send it yourself, to the recipient you choose.
- **Share to an AI app.** You can share a ready-made question (the facts of the purchase: item, store, date, price, fault description and demand) to an AI app you choose yourself. The app sends nothing by itself. Names, addresses and receipts are not included. What the other app does with the text is governed by that app's own terms.
- **PDF and CSV.** You save or share the file yourself.

## Pro purchase

Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. Payment is made with Google under Google's terms and privacy policy. We do not receive your payment details.

## Permissions

- Notifications: for deadline reminders, created on the phone.
- No camera, storage, location or contacts permission. Photos are picked with the system photo picker and camera.
- No internet permission.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data.

## Your choices and rights

Because we hold no data about you, we cannot correct or delete it. You control everything yourself: delete a purchase in the app, or uninstall the app, and everything stored locally is deleted. Files you exported yourself are deleted where you saved them.

## Changes

If any of this changes, the date above is updated.

The app gives guidance on consumer rights but not legal advice.
