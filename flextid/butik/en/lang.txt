Flextime keeps track of your working hours and flextime balance, with no account and no internet.

Clock in and out
One tap when you arrive and one when you leave, in the app, from the home screen widget or straight from Quick Settings with the Clock in tile. Your break is filled in automatically and can be changed. Forgot to clock in? Fix the day afterwards.

When can I go home?
During the day the app shows when your normal hours are done, and when your balance reaches zero if you are ahead or behind.

Balance and week
Set your own normal hours and working weekdays. Shorter Fridays or summer hours? Normal hours can differ per weekday and during a period every year. The app calculates your balance and shows the week day by day. Flex days off (taken from your balance), vacation, sick days and other leave are one choice away. The home screen widget shows your balance at a glance. The week and month show hours worked against normal hours, and you can share the week as text with any app. Set a maximum plus and minus for your balance and the app tells you when you are outside them.

Swedish public holidays and vacation
Swedish public holidays are built in and shown in the week. They have no normal hours, so working on one adds to your balance, and you get no clock-in reminder. Midsummer, Christmas and New Year's Eve can count as days off. The app shows vacation days taken, planned and left in the vacation year.

Reminders
Optional reminders to clock in in the morning and to clock out when your normal hours are done.

Report and export
Monthly PDF report with the month's normal hours, CSV for your own spreadsheet and a backup you save wherever you like. Export and backup are always free.

Privacy
No internet permission, no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no location. Everything stays on your phone.

Free and Pro
The free version has every feature and keeps up to 15 days. Pro is a one-time purchase that removes the limit. Nothing is deleted.

The app calculates from your own settings and gives no advice on what agreements or law allow.
