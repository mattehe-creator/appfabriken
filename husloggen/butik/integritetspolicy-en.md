# Privacy policy — Husloggen (House Log)

Last updated: 2026-10-21

Husloggen is developed by TM Connect. Contact: [contact address added at publication].

## Short version

Everything you save in Husloggen stays on your phone. We have no server, no account, no ads and no analytics. The app does not request internet permission and therefore cannot send anything by itself. We do not receive, store or share any personal data.

## What is stored, and where

The following is stored only locally on your phone, in the app's private storage:

- Homes: name, address if you enter one, and notes.
- Log entries: work done, date, amount, tax deductions, contractor and notes.
- Recurring tasks and planned costs.
- Meters and readings.
- Receipts and photos you take or pick, and documents (such as manuals and drawings) you add.
- Settings, such as reminders and the backup interval.

## When data leaves the phone

Only when you choose to:

- **Export and backup.** You save a file through the system file picker, to a location you choose, for example your own cloud service. Automatic backup is off by default and only writes to the folder you choose.
- **PDF and CSV.** You save or share the file yourself.
- **Opening a photo or document.** The file opens in an app you choose.

## Buying Pro

Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. Payment is made with Google under Google's terms and privacy policy. We never receive your payment details.

## Permissions

- Notifications: for reminders about recurring tasks, created on the phone.
- No camera, storage, location or contacts permission. Photos and documents are picked with the system photo picker, camera and file picker.
- No internet permission.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data.

## Your choices and rights

Since we hold no data about you, we cannot correct or delete any. You are in control: delete an entry in the app, or uninstall the app, and everything stored locally is deleted. Files you have exported yourself are deleted where you saved them.

## Changes

If any of this changes, the date above is updated.
