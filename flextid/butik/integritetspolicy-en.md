# Privacy policy — Flextid (Flex Time)

Last updated: 2026-10-21

Flextid is developed by TM Connect. Contact: [contact address added at publication].

## Short version

Everything you save in Flextid stays on your phone. We have no server, no account, no ads and no analytics. The app does not request internet permission and therefore cannot send anything by itself. We do not receive, store or share any personal data. The app does not know where you are and does not read your location.

## What is stored, and where

The following is stored only locally on your phone, in the app's private storage:

- Days: start and end time, break, type of day and note.
- Settings: normal hours, working days, opening balance and reminders.

## When data leaves the phone

Only when you choose to:

- **Export and backup.** You save a file (CSV, PDF or backup) through the system file picker, to a location you choose.
- **Sharing.** You share a file yourself, with a recipient you choose.

## Buying Pro

Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. Payment is made with Google under Google's terms and privacy policy. We never receive your payment details.

## Permissions

- Notifications: for reminders to clock in and out, created on the phone.
- No location, camera, storage or contacts permission.
- No internet permission.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data.

## Your choices and rights

Since we hold no data about you, we cannot correct or delete any. You are in control: delete a day in the app, or uninstall the app, and everything stored locally is deleted. Files you have exported yourself are deleted where you saved them.

## Changes

If any of this changes, the date above is updated.

The app calculates from your own settings and gives no advice on what agreements or law allow.
